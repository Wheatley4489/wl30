WL-30SX

Key guide

The top line shows what you type. The bottom line shows the answer after ENTER.

Second functions
Tap 2nd, then a key, to use the gold label printed above it.
Negative numbers
Use (−) for a negative sign. The − key is subtraction only.
Previous entries
▲ ▼ scroll back through past entries. ENTER reruns one; ◀ ▶ edit it. Starting with an operator uses the last answer.
Editing
Typing overwrites the character under the cursor. 2nd INS switches to insert. DEL removes a character.
Settings
DRG picks DEG, RAD or GRD. 2nd SCI/ENG picks the number format and 2nd FIX sets decimal places. Move with ◀ ▶ and press ENTER.
Fractions
3 A b/c 1 A b/c 4 enters 3 1/4. 2nd F◂▸D switches the answer between fraction and decimal.
Angles and coordinates
° ′ ″ holds degree, minute, second, radian and grad marks and ▸DMS. 2nd R◂▸P converts between rectangular and polar.
Memory
5 STO▸ pick A–E, ENTER. MEMVAR shows the stored values and pastes one; 2nd CLRVAR clears them.
Constant
2nd K, type an operation such as ×2, ENTER. Each number you enter then gets ×2. Press 2nd K again to stop.
Statistics
2nd STAT picks 1-VAR or 2-VAR. DATA opens the list; type a value and press ▼. STATVAR shows n, x̄, Sx, σx and the regression a, b, r.
Probability
PRB holds nPr, nCr, factorial, RAND and RANDI. RANDI takes two whole numbers separated by 2nd ,.
Hardware keyboard
Digits, + − * / ^ ( ), Enter, Backspace, arrow keys and Esc for CLEAR.
